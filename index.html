🌾 Feed formulation & preparation

Mix your own feed from local ingredients. Numbers below are practical starting points for Ghana; adjust with your vet officer or extension agent and your actual ingredient analysis.

🐦 Quail targets

Quail need more protein than chickens and start laying early — around 6–8 weeks. Numbers below follow published quail requirements.

FeedAgeCrude proteinNotes
Starter0–6 wks24%Fine crumble; chicks are tiny from day one
Grower6–8 wks20–22%Bridge to layer feed
Layer6 wks +18%Calcium 2.5–3.0%, available phosphorus about 0.38%
Why less protein at lay?Trials on laying Japanese quail found 18% crude protein supported the highest hen-day production. Do not push 24% layer feed at laying birds — it wastes money.

🐦 Guinea fowl feeding stages

Ghana research (Okyere et al., keets in southern Ghana) found keets thrive on 24% crude protein with 12.5 MJ ME/kg, a protein-to-energy ratio of about 19 g/MJ.

🔬 What research actually supports

Black soldier fly larvae mealBroiler trials find 20–25% inclusion partly replacing fishmeal supports growth without hurting performance; some studies show up to 75% replacement works when diets are balanced. Cut fishmeal cost with it.
Phytase enzymeReleases phosphorus locked in plant phytate, so you buy less bone meal and dicalcium phosphate.
Methionine first, then lysineIn maize-soy diets these are the limiting amino acids. 0.1–0.15% methionine usually pays for itself in growth.
Aflatoxin binderBentonite or yeast-cell-wall binders protect the liver and growth. Essential with Ghanaian maize and groundnut cake in humid storage.
Probiotics and prebioticsImproved gut health and lower mortality in trial conditions; cheap insurance in hot climates.
Feed formCrumble for keets and chicks raises intake. Wet mash at dawn helps birds eat before the midday heat in Tamale.
Heat-stress supportAbove 32°C add vitamin C and electrolytes, feed early morning and evening, keep cool water always available.

🧪 Preparation steps

1. Buy dryMaize and groundnut below 12% moisture: it should crack, not squash, and smell sweet, never musty.
2. Treat soybeansToast or roast full-fat soybeans until light brown to destroy trypsin inhibitor. Raw beans depress growth.
3. Grind evenlyFiner grind for keets and chicks, coarser for layers. Uneven grind lets birds sort and waste feed.
4. Pre-mix the small stuffMix premix, salt and minerals into about 5 kg of maize first, then blend into the full batch. Never dump 250 g of premix straight into 100 kg.
5. Mix 10–15 minutesUntil colour is uniform. Shovel from the bottom of the heap back to the top three times.
6. Store rightSacks on pallets, off the floor, dry room, use within 3 weeks. Label each sack with the formula and date.

💰 Cut the cost

Buy maize at harvestOctober to December prices are lowest in the north. Dry and store properly rather than buying in the lean season.
Group purchasePremix, methionine and fishmeal are cheaper by the carton. Buy with other farmers and split.
Formulate to current pricesRe-check ingredient prices monthly: when soybean meal jumps, BSF meal and groundnut cake become worth more.
Weigh, never estimateGuesswork is the biggest hidden cost in home mixing. A GH₵60 kitchen scale saves sacks of feed.

⚠️ Safety

Never feed mouldy grainAflatoxin causes liver damage, poor growth and death. No binder fully rescues badly spoiled grain.
Respect withdrawalStop medicated feed and coccidiostats before slaughter and before selling eggs, per the label or your vet.
Avoid risky by-productsRaw cassava (cyanide), cottonseed (gossypol) and restaurant waste (disease) are not worth the risk.
Clean water beats feedBirds drink about twice what they eat. Dirty water undoes a good ration faster than a poor one does.

Educational guidance only. Ingredient nutrient values vary, so confirm formulations with a veterinary officer or extension agent before scaling up.
